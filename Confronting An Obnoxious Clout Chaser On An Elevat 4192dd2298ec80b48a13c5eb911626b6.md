I was walking through the wide hallway of an underground shopping mall with my parents as we stood in front of a three elevators about to call the middle one. The doors of the middle elevator suddenly opened and a fat man wearing grey clothes with a poo stain on the back of his pants fell onto to the ground outside of the elevator and rolled around a bit.

> **Man**: Wait, let me take a selfie. I want to get back on the elevator, hold on.
> 

We didn’t know how to react and I didn’t want to put up with him so we boarded the elevator and I hurriedly pressed the button for the street level. Shortly after the doors closed they reopened and a crowd was surrounding and tending to the man. A woman with grey hair in front of the doors spoke to me.

> **Woman**: Can you wait for this man to board the elevator.
> 

I ignored her and pressed the button to ascend again. While the doors were closing an athletic elderly woman dived onto the doorway and crawled into the elevator as she was almost squeezed by the doors. The elevator ascended to the street level, we found our car, and drove off.
