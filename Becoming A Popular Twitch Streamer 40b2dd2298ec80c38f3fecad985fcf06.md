I was streaming for the first time on Twitch with a black screen despite my social anxiety. Eventually, my mum called and I discussed something with her while she didn’t realize that we were live. I don’t remember the conversation. 96 people started watching the stream because of how peculiar it was. There was a system in place where the total viewers was capped at 100 and it had to be upgraded through gifted subs.

> **Chat**: Is that your mum?
Mum.
> 

I considered how ridiculously easy it must be to become popular on Twitch because people would watch anything if it was different and interesting. When the phone call ended I explained to the chat my problem.

> **Me**: Sorry everyone that is it for today. I’m going to stop streaming now. It’s difficult to stream anything at the moment because of university. I’ll be back at some point in the future when I’m available.
> 

> **Chat**: Bye.
Cya.
> 

My mum bought a triangular rotating black cosmetic display with glasses one side, and clothes on the other sides which she placed in my room. I was annoyed because I didn’t want it and it was making it harder to walk around the room which made me feel claustrophobic.

The next day I started streaming on Twitch again as a lot of people started watching and I was playing an FPS videogame when I decided to do a trickshot. As I got more attention I became more confident and my social anxiety went away.

> **Me**: Are you all ready to see something truly magical?
> 

I fired a burst shot of three bullets and moved my mouse to perfectly headshot three enemy players in front of me who were spaced apart and they fell down dead. The chat went wild and was suspicious.

> **Chat**: What!
No way.
W.
Pog.
Hacker.
Hacks!
> 

I noticed the allegations and paused the videogame to defend myself.

> **Me**: I’m not hacking. Clip the video right now and watch it frame by frame. That is pure talent.
> 

> **Chat**: Want to make a bet?
Bet?
> 

> **Me**: I’ll bet you 10 subscribers that I’m not hacking.
> 

The viewers liked me because of how adamant and skilled I was. I started to periodically receive gifted subs from different viewers of varying amounts like 2, 3 and 5 which interrupted my conversations and I paused immediately to thank each donator.
