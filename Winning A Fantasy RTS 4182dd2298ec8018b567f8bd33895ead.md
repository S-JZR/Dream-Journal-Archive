I was in an antique magic shop which was actually my year 7 classroom at primary school (which was behind it in the dream) playing Beyond All Reason with 2 teams except with a fantasy theme and all the units were toys. The building featured a long bench below the whiteboard and all of the desks in a 5x4 formation at the right of the room.

The enemy team was camping to the right of the building which prompted me to make a basic base with a heavy lazer tower and produced diverse tier 1 units including goblins, armoured goblins, red creatures with a metal shoulder blade with cylinder bumps that shot fire, and orange glasses. All of produced units fell into a rectangular wire mesh waste bin. A female team member warned me about what the enemy was doing.

> **Woman**: The enemy team has constructed a city.
> 

> **Me**: We need to attack now or it will be too late.
> 

I saw my team members sending lots of units that walked in a line past the tuckshop to the year 7 block. I picked up the waste bin and moved to the same spot where I saw a large mini rectangular city in the year 7 eating area. I put on the pair of orange glasses which made it easier to see.

> **Me**: Now I can see everything.
> 

My team members focused on making tier 2 units but I made lots of tier 1 units. The enemy had three different x2 size super monsters that merged different things together such as a red unit with a metal spiked ball fist.

I walked through the city and repeatedly sprinkled a handful of units everywhere. Destroying the buildings rewarded us resources.

I went back to my base to build construction turrets and mass produce windmills on the desks. I was worried about the enemy doing a surprise attack to destroy my vulnerable base so I instantly upgraded the heavy tower to level 3 and placed another one. The level 3 tower looked like a plush featuring a cube and square frustrum with a rotating pyramid on top with a black all seeing eye on the front side.

I remembered that I needed to set all of my units to roam to ensure they would continue attacking everything. I walked to the frontline and realized that we destroyed everything and won.
