I was with many adults walking through a long wide concrete hallway filled with scattered elevated square slabs. There was a fast dark grey monster resembling The Lifeform from The Backrooms that always spawned from behind. The environment periodically cycled between a safe phase where the monster de-spawned and an unsafe phase where the monster spawned to hunt anyone not standing on a slab. At some point everyone stood on slabs preemptively except a man who was far away from slabs ahead of him and was chased by the monster as everyone watched.

> **People**: Run run! It’s behind you!
> 

He sprinted to a slab and got on just in time as the monster was about to grab him.

At the end of the hallway there was a giant mechanical contraption in the centre that repeatedly lifted and dropped two circular steel plates at a 45 degree angle. The ground was depressed under both plates and lined with a single layer of dark grey mannequins. A naive woman decided to hide under the right plate instead of continuing to exit the hallway.

> **Man**: Don't hide there, you will get caught!
> 

She was temporarily pinned down when the monster spawned, found her anyway, and attacked her.
