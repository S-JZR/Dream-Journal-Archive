I was watching Markiplier live streaming himself playing a third-person base building wave survival zombie videogame. He already did some work building a base and was a few waves in. Each wave different types of zombies spawned, those outside the facility were were weaker but more numerous, and those inside were super strong but scarce. Super strong zombies could infiltrate the facility if you didn’t secure every part of it properly. He was playing as an athletic male character. He had a facecam in the top-left corner of the frame. He was standing in front of a 3 metre tall concrete wall outside of an industrial facility when a horde of zombies spawned and approached him. He climbed over the wall as the zombies tried to grab him.

> **Markiplier**: No no you will not get me.
> 

He ran into the front hallway of the facility where he triggered a tripwire that opened two square holes on both sides of the room with sticky pistons and lava started flowing out like Minecraft which completely stopped the horde.

> **Markiplier**: Lava.
> 

He came to a T-intersection hallway and moved to two open doors on the right that lead to the central area of the facility but he discovered it was overrun by super zombies. A tall grey zombie with thin pointed limbs was sprinting with flailing limbs towards the doors and he had nowhere to go so he stood to the left of the doors idly as he hugged the wall and it ran past without noticing him. He had a shocked expression and continued entering the area.

Inside the area there was a white zombie with stacked crates surrounding it in a circular area with gaps between them and it randomly rotated left and right trying to detect players in its line of sight. He approached the right side of it and it happened to turn left so he moved past it.

He approached a giant power chip on the opposite side of the area plugged into the wall and pulled it out.
