I was holding an open coca cola can and a hot dog with the bottom half wrapped in wrapping paper standing in front of a long public pool with yellow walls which featured an external platform on the right side side divided by a wall. The pool was divided into multiple lanes. There was no way to get to the other platform so I decided to walk directly through the pool and hold up my arms. I didn’t hold up my arms high enough and the bottom half of the hotdog got wet. A male lifeguard wearing a red shirt standing in the same lane as me noticed me.

> **Man**: You’re not meant to take food and drinks in here. Your hotdog got wet, what are you going to do about it?
> 

> **Me**: I will probably wash it off or tear the pieces off.
> 

I continued through the pool until I exited onto the other platform.
