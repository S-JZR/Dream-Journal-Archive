I was standing in the lounge room of my unit with my family when a super poltergeist started to manifest in my upstairs bedroom and my dad randomly felt sick. We were about to take my dad to the hospital and my mum turn off the light and shut the door to my upstairs bedroom. I was looking upstairs when she descended and I saw the door open again and the light turn back on but there was nobody upstairs.

> **Me**: The light just turned back on upstairs.
> 

> **Mum**: Who turned it on again?
> 

> **Me**: There’s a demon up there.
> 

My mum went upstairs again to fix it and I followed here. There was a banjo temporarily being stored on the top of my bunk bed which played the “Minecraft” song from Minecraft by itself. I thought that it was directly targeting me since I’m the only one in my family who has played Minecraft.

> **Me**: Oh no. The banjo is playing by itself and it actually sounds good.
> 

We went back downstairs again to explain the situation.

> **Me**: There is a poltergeist upstairs.
> 

When my mum and I went upstairs again, all of the furniture was knocked over or flipped upside down.

> **Me**: What the heck.
> 

I tried to cast out the demon but it did nothing.

> **Me**: I rebuke you demon and command you to leave in the name of Jesus. You’re not allowed to return to this unit, unit complex, and neighbourhood.
> 

The poltergeist started to emit a dark fog that covered the entire room so we retreated downstairs.

A fictional female neighbour from unit 4 was influenced by the poltergeist to be abnormally frantic and she repeatedly kept begging me to take care of her grey cat but I was initially hesitant.

> **Woman**: Please take care of my cat.
> 

> **Me**: We can keep it for a while.
> 

> **Woman**: Thank you so much!
> 

I put the cat in my unit and it roamed.

A fictional male neighbour overheard the commotion and approached the front door.

> **Man**: Can I have a look?
> 

> **Me**: Sure.
> 

The man walked halfway up the stairs when he immediately collapsed and felt sick. I considered that perhaps my family has developed a tolerance to the demon if we had secretly been exposed long-term. We pulled my neighbour onto the sidewalk in front of my unit to safety.

We looked at the upstairs windows of my bedroom and noticed large yellow cat eyes blinking in the bottom right corner of the glass and everything behind the glass was black. I called 000 and a woman answered.

> **Woman**: What is your emergency?
> 

> **Me**: [Recalling the situation]. I think we’re going to have to perform an exorcism on my unit.
>
