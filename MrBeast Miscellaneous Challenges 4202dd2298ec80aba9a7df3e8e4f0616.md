I was one of many people on a MrBeast gameshow competing in team-based challenges. MrBeast made everyone fill out a survey full of random questions to throw them off guard but was really about discovering whether they were afraid of heights.

In the first challenge, we were in Minecraft and had to swim up a 1 block pillar of water to a “boat” in the sky which was really just a long rock with wood at the bottom then mine a tunnel through it to the other side. Inside the tunnel Chandler was drowning and rapidly placed torches to create an air pocket.

In the second challenge, we were in Minecraft in an underground pool area illuminated by ceiling sunlight and surrounded by polished granite and terrain. The pool was marked with red lines at regular intervals across its length with one yellow line. Everyone was assigned into separate teams and took turns answering one question to move up one increment but if they answered anything wrong they moved all the way back to the start. When one team stood on the yellow line, a female individual who was disadvantaged in the previous challenge was given the choice to answer an extra question to move forward or stay where there were. They opted to answer the question but got it wrong and move back to the start. Eventually, a team won the challenge.

In the third challenge, we were in the outdoor lunch area under an awning at my secondary school. Everyone was assigned to two teams. There was an escape room maze inside the hall with five rooms embedded in it and the walls were covered in dark grey pixelated camouflage. The challenge involved each team completing sub tasks to escape the maze including finding a key in a yellow box on a shelf to unlock a safe without being caught by someone dressed as a monster. The monster resembled a bulky figure with lots of strands of camouflaged ribbon hanging off all over its body and distorted teeth and black eyes. The monster peeked around the corner of the hall outside at the contestants who saw it as they were seated the stainless steel tables.

> **Woman**: Oh, I don’t like that.
> 

Eventually, a team won the challenge.

In the fourth challenge, there were pipes with screw-threaded ends on the ceiling of the awning outside of the hall.

> **MrBeast**: This next challenge involves those who are not afraid of heights being raised up and racing to see who can screw a cap onto the pipes on the ceiling the fastest. Can one person from each team come forward who is not afraid of heights.
> 

Two people came to the front.

> **MrBeast**: Except that would be too easy so instead we’re going to send those who are afraid of heights based on what everyone said in the surveys you all took at the start.
> 

> **Contestants**: *Gasp*
> 

> **MrBeast**: Decide amongst yourselves who you will send.
> 

Both teams sent various candidates and they took turns being raised on a scissor lift resembling a white hospital bed but got scared halfway up and came back down. One team had a quick pep talk.

> **Man**: You need to stop listening to your emotions and trust that the safety harness I put on you will keep you safe. I'll be there with you the whole time and make sure you won’t fall. Focus on the task and don’t look down.
> 

That team won because a team member focused on the task and never looked down.
