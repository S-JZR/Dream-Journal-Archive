I was sleeping in my bed in my unit at night when I heard a missile moving rapidly in the sky, hitting an aircraft, and exploding loudly. My mum ran up the stairs and stood in front of the doorway to my bedroom.

> **Mum**: Australia is experiencing some kind of crisis! Get up and get dressed!
> 

My brother and I hurriedly got out of bed and started putting our clothes on. While getting dressed I pulled out my phone and Googled “Australia emergency” to see if there were any news reports about it but there was nothing so I figured that perhaps this was a surprise attack by a foreign country.

I overheard the deep vibration of a plane flying around in the distance and opened my clothes cupboard to take with me a long rectangular retail box for a personal self-deploying semi-autonomous anti-air sentry in case we were ambushed. The box contained a collapsed tripod sentry equipped with 30 anti-air rounds that fired slowly and a VR controller with a ring to manually designate targets with a lazer.

I entered unit 5 because I realized that the appliances and lights were left on. In the kitchen I noticed a fictional male and female neighbour standing inside and an instant food machine on the counter. The machine resembled a microwave but with a large glass main compartment and four display cases with packets to the right accompanied by buttons on top. I selected the smallest packet then hot steaks and various food packages instantly materialized inside the compartment. I sprinted throughout the unit and turned off every appliance and light that I could find when my dad got angry and called out to me from the front door.

> **Dad**: [Me] Why are you taking so long?! We’re all waiting for you!
> 

I realized that I was running out of time and went underneath the stairs to flick off the main switch on the power board to completely shutoff all power.

I exited into the carpark when I looked into the distance and noticed various subtle fires in vegetation everywhere as pillars of black smoke arose.

> **Me**: Look at that over there.
> 

> **Mum**: *gasp*
> 

The pillars of smoke continued appearing until encircling our neighbourhood.

My dad, brother, and I waited outside as my mum backed the car out of the garage and into the driveway. I went into the paddock and experimented with anti-air sentry where I pushed an on button that make it unfold, scanned the sky, and fired a few rounds. We were about to get in my mum’s car when she drove off without us to the right.

> **Dad**: Where does she think she’s going?
> 

We chased after my mum’s car around the corner until we got to the T-junction and she went right. There were destroyed cars blocking the road and various rough-looking people burning things and looting to the left. My brother ran into the distance down the left road.

> **Dad**: Why is he going that way?
> 

We walked down the road to the right where we found my mum’s car idling and we got inside including my brother who returned then we left.
