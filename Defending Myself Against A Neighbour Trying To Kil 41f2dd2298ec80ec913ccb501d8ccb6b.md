I was standing in the lounge room of my unit when I overheard a male police officer talking through his patrol car’s PA system outside. Many neighbours including myself went outside to listen.

> **Man 1**: Attention residents. Wild bushfires have been reported in the area. Please hose down any dry bark and leaves around your properties to prevent them from catching fire.
> 

I started to water a strip of ground on the other side of my unit but a fictional male neighbour from unit 2 yelled at me.

> **Man 2**: Stop watering! Keep going and I’ll tell the community about this.
> 

After returning inside I overheard someone digging with a shovel and walked to the back door where I noticed the same neighbour digging underneath the fence and trying to get inside our yard. I approached my mum who was seated at the dining table doing banking on the phone and writing things down on a notepad. My dad was sitting on the sofa.

> **Me**: Ring 000! [Recalling the situation].
> 

My mum did nothing. I heard the backdoor rattle and realized the neighbour was trying to break in. I went to the kitchen and armed myself with two cooking knives. My neighbour broke the locks and approached me holding a knife. I stabbed him about 30 times in the chest as I walked backwards to the sofa and dodged his knife swings. When we reached the sofa I passed out onto it from fear and woke up later but he was gone.

> **Me**: What happened?
> 

> **Dad**: He retreated and fell unconscious.
