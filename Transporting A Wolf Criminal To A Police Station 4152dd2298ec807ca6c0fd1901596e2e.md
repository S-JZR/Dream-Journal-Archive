I was parked beside an empty highway with six lanes with a dark blue helicopter parked sideways across the central lanes nearby. An anthropomorphic wolf criminal that was highly wanted appeared and I tied them up in white strips of clothe and handcuffed them to the back of the cabin. I flew to a police station and landed inside the building in front of the holding cell featuring prison bars. I sprinted to the reception desk where various police officers were scattered and standing idly. I spoke to the nearest male police officer.

> **Me**: You know that wolf that is super wanted?
> 

> **Man**: Yeah?
> 

> **Me**: I caught him and he’s outside right now. Look for yourself he's right there!
> 

> **Man**: Sure you did…
> 

I walked outside to the prison cells and the male police officer followed me from behind. When he noticed the wolf on the helicopter he was shocked.

> **Man**: He really is here!
> 

All of the police officers overheard the shouting and hurried outside where they ensured the wolf was properly secured in the cell. The cell was already occupied by a white snake and green crocodile prisoner. Different police officers took shifts ensuring he didn’t try to escape by sitting on a wooden chair against the wall opposite the cells and keeping watch. There was also someone watching through a security camera in the right corner of the ceiling.
