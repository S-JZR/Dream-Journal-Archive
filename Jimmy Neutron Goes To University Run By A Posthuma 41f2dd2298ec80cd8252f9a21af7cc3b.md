I assumed the role of Jimmy Neutron who was going to university for the first time along with his male friend. He also had an A4 yellow folder at home which functioned as a scrapbook with about 7 pages in it. He used rocket boots to travel to university and left them on the front sidewalk, leaning against a cobblestone wall. He entered the lecture theatre as the female vice chancellor was giving a speech but then she slandered Jimmy.

> **Woman**: It’s rather disappointing to have someone like Jimmy here with us. We were inspecting their files recently and discovered a troubling photograph concealed at the end.
> 

The whiteboard projector showed a recording of someone opening his A4 folder to the end and unfolding a page which revealed the polaroid photo of a black girl on it and I instinctively knew he was being framed. I assume that they were trying to accuse him of being a paedophile which completely ruined all of his credibility.

> **Students**: *Gasp*.
> 

> **Jimmy’s Friend**: I’ve looked through that folder many times and I have never seen that there! It must have been planted!
> 

At the end of the session all of the other students left except for Jimmy and his friend. Various cult members wearing brown robes unexpectedly emerged while chanting various phrases and chased after them by walking. They fled into a 13-storey op shop to the left of the theatre and ascended to the top floor on a ladder where they entered the attic via a manhole in the centre of the ceiling and locked it with a latch. However, the cult members still found them, broke into the attic, and brought them to the stage in the theatre where they forced Jimmy to sit back in an operating chair at a 45-degree angle.

> **Woman**: I’m going to create a new digital race of people far better than the useless members of our society who are so slow and dull.
> 

The vice chancellor and a male supervisor held him down with one hand on his head and hip. She used her other hand to place a single suction cup electrode onto his forehead and reached for more on a stainless steel Mayo stand. I instinctively knew she was going to brainwash him if she finished setting everything up. Jimmy picked up a metal tool off the stand and stripped the end of the electrode wire which he pressed onto her face to electrocute them all, including himself, since they were still holding him. He continued until the vice chancellor went limp and also jabbed the supervisor to break free. He fled to the front of the university as everyone chased him, strapped on the rocket boots he had left earlier, and streaked down the road to escape.
