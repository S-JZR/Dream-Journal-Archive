I walked into the upstairs bathroom in my unit when I noticed that my mum installed automatic orange tap system that was very glitchy featuring:

- A tap nozzle that used a motion sensor to abruptly swivel to the location of my hands. It often painfully collided with my hands.
- Tap handles that snap to 45 degree rotations. Giving them a little nudge was enough to make them rotate.
- A water fountain on a square table with a square top that had a hole which could be rotated 360 degrees for 4 different modes, one mode made water come out of the fountain and the other three seemed to do nothing. There was a glass window that allowed you to see water flowing inside and anything that didn’t come out directly flowed into the drain.
- Miscellaneous rectangular buttons in front of the tap handles and water fountain that set them to specific modes. The button to turn on the water fountain made it auto rotate into position but no water came out unlike manually rotating it.

I told my brother about it who was sitting at his computer in his room and he inspected it.
