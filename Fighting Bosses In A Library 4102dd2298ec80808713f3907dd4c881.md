I was in a large old wooden library with a tall ceiling and lots of separate rooms with a few other adults playing an RPG videogame. All bosses had a red health bar that spanned the full width of the HUD at the top of the frame. We entered through the front hallway into the first side room.

In the first side room, there were bookshelves on the left and right walls. A skeleton boss x1.5 the height of a person spawned and we all individually started fighting it. Most people hit it with swords which made it flicker red from damage. I was holding a jug of holy water and cast it onto the skeleton but it had no effect. Eventually, the boss died and we entered the adjacent room.

In the second side room, there were stainless steel cone pendant lights hanging from the ceiling on long wires. A boss was about to spawn so we climbed onto the ceiling lights for our own safety and hung off the side as we huddled together. The boss was a giant flying white snake with a human skull and eyes.

> **Man**: Not this boss!
> 

The boss began flying toward us so we rapidly ascended the light at different heights to avoid being attacked as it brushed past. When it turned around most people were shooting arrows at it. I cast my jug of holy water onto its scaly skin which caused it to sizzle and melt off. I also pulled out a camera which had a green button that charged up a flash and an orange button that took a photo. I held the camera directly in front of its right eye and the boss turned its eyes to look at me. I took a photo but it had no effect other than turning its sclera orange. I tried to flash it eyes three times but I couldn’t time the button presses properly and could only flash 1 time. A man entered from the front hallway holding a bunch of chocolates, including a Toblerone bar, and dropped a long chocolate bar on the floor. We all fled further into the library as the boss was about to chase us but noticed the chocolate and stopped to eat it which was gooey and took a long time.

In the third room, it looked like a square study hall:

- Centre:
    - Six desks with green Banker's lamps on them.
- Front:
    - Two doors with separate hallways (where we came from)
- Left:
    - A door to a hallway.
- Back:
    - Stairs that lead to an office with windows.

We ascended the stairs into the office and watched other people, including my parents exiting the left door when they were about to go where we came from.

> **Everyone**: Be careful, we encountered a boss back there!
> 

They noticed something that was outside our field of view and fled back through the the door to the left. A man exited the front right door. A boss resembling a woman wearing a dark blue cloak holding a syringe exited from the front left door and chased after the man.

> **Everyone**: The boss is chasing you, run!
>
