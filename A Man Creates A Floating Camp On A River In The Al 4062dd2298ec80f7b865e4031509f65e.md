I was spectating a man being filmed for a survival TV show who was standing on a floating camp he setup on a river in the alps using 1x10 rectangular light-grey textured rigid foam mats that can be connected together on all sides.

> **Man**: Normally in an environment with these harsh conditions nobody would dare to live here but I'm going to defy the odds and do it anyway which is why I built this floating camp.
> 

I felt concerned that his main area was too narrow and he risked accidentally rolling off into the water when he slept during the night and drowning from hypothermia. The man walked down to the end of the line of mats, detached two of them, and walked back to reassemble them in a 1x3 area to place his equipment and sleeping bag on top.
